About TENKOU-TK

Telkom University–Kyoto University Environmental & Kinetic Observation Unit

The project

TENKOU-TK is a web map that brings together public CCTV streams, weather information and road intersection data to observe traffic and environmental conditions in Bandung, Indonesia.

Live camera map
Public CCTV streams across the city, with their online status and a multi-camera wall per road corridor.
Weather and flood watch
Rainfall, wind and river discharge layers, together with monitoring of flood-prone locations.
Spatial analysis
Road intersections, camera clusters, density heatmaps and terrain elevation.
Traffic and weather forecast
An experimental page for Terusan Buah Batu with live video. It replays recorded data from 20–26 November 2026 with forecasts up to six hours ahead.
